FAQ

How do I recover my account?

If you signed in with Google or Apple, your game data is linked to that account. Sign in with the same account on any device to restore your progress.

I'm playing as a guest. Will I lose my progress?

A guest account is tied to your current device. Link it to a Google or Apple account in the in-game Settings menu to keep your progress safe and play on other devices. If you delete the Game or change devices before linking, guest progress cannot be recovered.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from the in-game Settings menu. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

I made a purchase but didn't receive my items.

Open the in-game Settings menu and tap "Restore" next to "Restore purchases". This will deliver any purchases that were paid for but not received. If the issue persists, contact us with your purchase receipt and we will resolve it promptly.

The app is crashing or not loading.

Please ensure your device is running iOS 17.0 or later (for iOS) or Android 8.0 or later. If the problem continues, contact us with your device model and OS version.

What is a season and when does it end?

Erathon runs in 7-day seasons. At the end of each season, rankings are finalized and your civilization starts over, while your Heroes stay with you. A brief maintenance period (1 hour) precedes each new season.

Does the Game keep producing while I'm away?

Yes. Your districts keep producing Gold, Tech, and Fame while the Game is closed, and everything you earned is added when you return.

Are there ads?

No. Erathon has no ads.
